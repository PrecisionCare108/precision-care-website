Feedback & complaints

We want to hear from you.

Feedback helps us understand what is working and where we can improve. Participants, families, representatives and other people can raise feedback or concerns with us.

Contact Precision Care

Email info@precisioncareandservices.com.au with the subject “Feedback or Complaint”. Tell us how you would prefer us to contact you and provide only the information you are comfortable sharing initially.

We aim to treat concerns respectfully, fairly and without affecting a person's right to receive appropriate support.

If you raise a concern, we will listen, respond respectfully and explain the next steps. You may also choose to have a representative assist you.
