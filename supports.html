Our supports

Support for everyday life and the goals ahead.

Supports are tailored to individual needs, goals, funding arrangements and availability.

Live More Independently

Everyday routines, household activities, shopping, meal preparation and practical tasks that support greater independence.

Get Out & Connect

Community participation shaped around interests — social activities, hobbies, shopping, local outings and new experiences.

Build Confidence & Life Skills

Support to develop practical skills, routines, confidence and independence at an individual pace.

Stay Active & Enjoy Your Community

Walking, recreation, libraries, events, sightseeing and other activities aligned with the participant's goals and interests.

Appointments & Everyday Tasks

Practical support to attend appointments, complete errands, manage household tasks and maintain everyday routines with greater confidence and independence.

Youth Justice Support

Individualised support for young people with disability who require structured, consistent support, including those involved with, or transitioning from, the youth justice system. We focus on building positive routines, emotional regulation, confidence, life skills and community participation while supporting pathways towards education, employment and greater independence. Our approach emphasises patience, trust, consistency and collaboration with families, carers and other professionals.

Consistent • structured • person-centred

Short-Term & Respite Support

Flexible support during temporary stays, changes in living arrangements or periods when additional assistance is needed. Support may include daily routines, meals, activities, appointments and community access.

Accommodation arrangements are discussed and agreed as part of each individual service arrangement. Precision Care does not represent accommodation as being owned or operated by the organisation unless specifically agreed.

Complex & Individualised Support

Tailored support for participants with complex needs who benefit from consistency, patience and a personalised approach. We work alongside participants, families, carers and allied health professionals to support communication, positive routines, independence, community participation and meaningful progress towards individual goals.

Complex needs · person-centred · collaborative
