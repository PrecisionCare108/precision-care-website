About Precision Care

Support built around the person.

Precision Care & Services Australia Pty Ltd provides practical, person-centred disability support focused on choice, dignity, consistency and participation in everyday life.

Our approach

We start by listening. Supports are shaped around the participant's goals, preferences, routines, interests and circumstances rather than a one-size-fits-all program.

Relationships matter

We value reliable support, familiar workers and genuine rapport. Trust and consistency can make everyday support more effective and comfortable.

Independence

Where appropriate, we work with participants rather than simply doing things for them, supporting confidence and practical skills over time.

Community

Meaningful participation can include everyday errands, hobbies, recreation, appointments, learning, social connection and exploring new interests.
