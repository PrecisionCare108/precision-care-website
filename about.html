ABOUT PRECISION CARE

Support built around the person.

Precision Care & Services Australia Pty Ltd provides personalised disability support under the NDIS focused on choice, dignity, consistency and meaningful participation in everyday life.

Person-centred support

We start by listening. Support is shaped around each participant’s goals, preferences, routines and interests rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Trusting relationships matter

Reliable support comes from genuine relationships. We value consistency, familiarity and taking the time to understand each person.

Building independence

We work alongside participants to develop confidence, practical skills and greater independence at their own pace.

Community connection

Meaningful participation can include everyday activities, hobbies, appointments, learning, recreation and building connections within the community.

OUR STORY

Why Precision Care exists.

Precision Care was founded on a simple belief: disability support should feel personal.

After working alongside people with disability and witnessing the difference that trust, consistency and genuine connection can create, Precision Care was established to focus on the person behind the support plan.

We believe good support is not about doing things for people. It is about working alongside them to build confidence, independence and meaningful participation in everyday life.

Our values

Respect and dignity • Choice and control • Trust and consistency • Independence • Community connection

Working with families and professionals

We collaborate with participants, families, Support Coordinators, Plan Managers and allied health professionals to create supports that are practical, consistent and aligned with individual goals.

Start with a conversation.

Whether you are a participant, family member, Support Coordinator or Plan Manager, we would welcome the opportunity to discuss how Precision Care can support your goals.
