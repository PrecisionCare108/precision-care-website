About us

Everyday support with a personal approach.

At Precision Care, we believe quality support starts with understanding the person behind the plan. We work alongside participants, families and support teams to create consistent, meaningful support that reflects individual goals, routines and choices.

Our supports

Start with what you want to achieve.

Daily Living & Independence

Support to build confidence with everyday routines, household tasks, meal preparation, shopping and practical skills that promote greater independence.

Community Access & Participation

Support to explore interests, attend appointments, enjoy activities and build meaningful connections within the community.

Youth Justice Support

Individualised support for young people with disability who are involved with, or transitioning from, the youth justice system. We focus on building positive routines, confidence, life skills, community participation and pathways towards meaningful goals.

Short-Term & Respite Support

Flexible support during changing circumstances, temporary arrangements or when additional assistance is needed with routines, activities and community participation.

Complex & Individualised Support

Tailored support for participants with complex needs requiring consistency, patience and a collaborative approach. We work alongside participants, families and allied health professionals to support communication, routines, independence and meaningful progress.
